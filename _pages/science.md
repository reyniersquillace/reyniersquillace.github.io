I work on pulsars and exotic stellar binaries. Since 2023, I've been a member of the CHAMPSS collaboration, a project on the CHIME radio telescope to perform a power spectrum search for new pulsars. CHAMPSS uses the transit nature of CHIME to stack many days' power spectra. For CHAMPSS, I developed a new approach to inject artificial signals into the power spectrum and probe our sensitivity, which will be presented in an upcoming paper. 

I'm particularly interested in pulsars with Main Sequence companions. How can we use their radio emission to study their companions? How can we use optical emission from their companions to study the pulsars? And how can we combine different observational strategies to break degenercies and understand the complex interactions in these systems? Stay tuned. :)
